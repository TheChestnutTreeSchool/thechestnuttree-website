Year 1–2 Curriculum

Long-term plan

Across six terms, Years 1 and 2 build English and Catalan/Castellano literacy and grammar, number and place value through geometry and measurement, and thematic units spanning art, geography, science, history, design & technology, and computing — alongside our "Diplomacy" strand (caring for others, gifts and giving, friendship, sacred places, volunteering) each term.

Literacy & Languages

Reading and writing in English, plus SPaG across English, Castellano, and Catalan — sentence structure, punctuation, adjectives, and word-building.

Maths

Number and place value, addition/subtraction, geometry, multiplication and division, fractions, and measurement.

Science & History

Seasons, materials, plants and animals, first inventors, first cities, and significant historical figures.

Art & Design

Colour, sculpture, fabric design, and studies of artists such as Joan Miró and L.S. Lowry.

Geography

Our local area, our country, seasonal weather, and the wider world.

Computing & Diplomacy

Early computing skills, online safety, ScratchJr programming — alongside caring for others, friendship, and volunteering themes.
