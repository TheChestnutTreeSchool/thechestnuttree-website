Outdoor Environmental Education Program

Welcome to The Chestnut Tree Ecological School's Outdoor Environmental Education Program.

About the program

Our program offers a wide range of engaging activities and hikes in the beautiful Montseny and Corredor Montnegre region. Students will have the opportunity to learn about forest ecology, local history, aquatic ecosystems, soil science, survival skills, and much more, through hands-on experiences in nature.

Inspiration

The program is inspired and designed by the work of geographer and naturalist Martí Boada, drawing on his archive of publications on the forests of Catalonia and the flora of Montseny.

Booking

Activities can be booked at the school or at Sanata (Llinars del Vallès)
