Ages 2–5

The first outdoor steps

Play-based learning across seven key areas, following England's Early Years Foundation Stage framework, delivered largely outdoors.

Our youngest students build language, physical skills, self-confidence, and environmental awareness through nature-based play — from listening to stories about the outdoors to planting seeds and observing seasonal change.

Communication & Language

Listening to nature stories, following outdoor instructions, describing what they observe on walks.

Physical Development

Climbing, balancing, using tools to plant and care for the garden, and outdoor hygiene routines.

Personal, Social & Emotional

Planting and recycling activities that build responsibility and respect for living things.

Literacy & Maths

Books and stories about nature; counting leaves, stones, flowers, and exploring shapes found outdoors.

Understanding the World

Seasonal changes, local habitats, simple eco-friendly technology, and how communities depend on nature.

Expressive Arts

Art from natural materials, imaginative play, and environmental storytelling, music, and dance.
