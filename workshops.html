Nature Workshops / Tallers

Hands-on learning in the beautiful Montseny region, connecting classroom learning with real-world ecological understanding. Each workshop is adaptable to all age groups, 10–25 participants per group.

All workshops

14 hands-on sessions across the Montseny region

3 hours

Corredor Montnegre Interpretive Hike

Forest ecosystem observation, traditional land use, biodiversity documentation, and historical site interpretation.

2 hours

Aquatic Study Workshop

Water sample collection, microscopic organism identification, food chain mapping.

2 hours

Soil Science Workshop

Soil profile excavation, porosity testing, and decomposition process observation.

3 hours

Survival Skills Workshop

Shelter construction, fire-making, water purification, and edible plant identification.

2 hours

Dendrochronology Workshop

Tree core sampling, ring counting, and climate correlation analysis.

2 hours

Leadership Team Building

Trust exercises, problem-solving challenges, and leadership rotation activities.

2 hours

Sensory Awareness Hike

Exploring the five senses through a blindfolded trail navigation experience.

2 hours total

Migration & Orienteering Skills

Animal migration patterns plus compass basics and navigation technique.

3 hours total

Scavenger Hunt & Squirrelympics

Map-reading challenges and games exploring squirrel adaptations.

3 hours total

Biodiversity & Bird Studies

Bioblitz species identification and a focused study of local bird morphology.

3 hours total

Plankton & Insect Studies

Why plankton matters for life on Earth, plus insect taxonomy and safe handling.

4 hours total

Natural Arts & Traditional Crafts

Natural pigments and chromatography, plus nature poetry and herbarium-making.

4 hours total

Human Footprint Workshop

Understanding ecological footprint through navigation, survival skills, and woodcraft.

Diplomacy, Peace & Community
