The Solidarity Fund

Ten percent of all Solidarity Fund income is automatically allocated to a dedicated emergency reserve, with the remainder supporting scholarships and campus development.

Where it goes

  • Scholarship places for families who could not otherwise access this education
  • Campus development and outdoor infrastructure
  • A dedicated emergency reserve (10% of all Fund income)

Donate

Get in touch about donating

We handle donations directly rather than through a generic payment button, so we can tell you exactly where your contribution goes. Interested families may also join the Solidarity Fund committee, which prepares an annual budget each school year.

Our Financial Philosophy

Three principles behind every contribution

The Chestnut Tree is more than a school: it is a community built on trust, respect, and mutual support. Each family's financial contribution is part of a broader collective effort.

Community Participation

Everyone can contribute to the School's success through time, commitment, or financial means.

Solidarity

Families have different economic circumstances; community support can help maintain inclusion.

Transparency

Operating costs and the use of family contributions are communicated openly, including at community meetings.
