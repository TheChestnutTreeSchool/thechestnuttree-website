Admissions

Applying starts with a visit, not a form.

We want every family to see a real morning at the school before deciding it's right for their child.

Step 1

Book a visit

Join us for a "Portes Obertes" (Open Doors) morning or arrange a private tour. You'll meet teachers, see a class in session, and get a feel for how the day actually runs.

  1. Send us your child's age and a few dates that work for you.
  2. We confirm a visit slot within 2 working days.
  3. After your visit, we send you the application pack.
