01 — 04

Socio-Ecological Pedagogy

Ruta Sentinelles del Bosc — our tribute to Dr. Martí Boada Juncà

Our Socio-Eco Pedagogy is rooted in the work and influence of Dr. Martí Boada Juncà — one of Catalonia's most recognised environmental scientists, naturalists, and educators, deeply connected to the Montseny massif, our school's UNESCO Biosphere Reserve home. He has mentored our founders since 2022. In his honour, our Baix Montseny campus hosts Ruta Sentinelles del Bosc, an on-site interpretive trail with four learning stations: dendrochronology, pedology, transect & herbarium, and topophilia.

02 — 04

Small by design

A 1:8 ratio, so teaching can stay personal

With one teacher for every eight children, our team teaches to the child in front of them, not a curriculum written for an average one. Most lessons happen outdoors, in all seasons, where questions come from the world itself rather than a worksheet.
