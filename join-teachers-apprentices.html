Understanding Socio-Eco Pedagogy

Inspired by Dr. Martí Boada

Socio-Eco Pedagogy is a transformative educational approach that connects human societies and natural ecosystems, moving beyond traditional environmental education to show how social structures, economic models, and cultural values impact ecological well-being.

Holistic Socio-Ecological Understanding

Recognizing the deep interdependence of natural, social, cultural, and economic systems.

Critical Reflection & Transformative Action

Combining critical reflection with transformative action toward systemic change.

Ethical Co-Responsibility & Sustainability

Emphasising intergenerational justice and sustainable lifestyles.

Global Citizenship & Community Care

Developing global responsibility and an ethic of care for all communities, human and non-human.

Transforming education for tomorrow

Holistic Development

Nurturing emotional intelligence, ecological awareness, and critical thinking for lifelong learning.

Global Partnership

Aligned with UNESCO frameworks, contributing to the worldwide sustainable education movement.

Future Leaders

Preparing students as environmental stewards and compassionate global citizens.
